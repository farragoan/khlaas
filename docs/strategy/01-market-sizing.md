## The Core Insight: You Are Not Competing with Splitwise

Splitwise is a **debt-tracking** app. khlaas is a **bill-splitting** app. These look the same but are structurally different markets.

- Splitwise is used **after** the meal: "I paid, you owe me."
- khlaas is used **at** the meal: "Let's figure out who owes what right now."

The Splitwise TAM is the ~150M people globally who have recurring split relationships (housemates, travel companions, long-term friend groups). khlaas's TAM is every group that **eats a meal together** — a 10× larger moment.

## Why the GCC is the Priority Beachhead

"Khlaas" (خلاص) is a **common Arabic colloquial expression** meaning "it's done / finished / sorted." It is used in daily speech in UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, Qatar, Egypt, and Lebanon. This is not a clever brand name in these markets — it is a **household word**.

The product name carries zero CAC in Arabic-speaking markets. A user in Dubai hearing "khlaas app" immediately understands the product before seeing a single screenshot.

Additional GCC structural advantages:
- **High group dining culture:** Friday brunches, large family gatherings, wasta-based hospitality culture where multiple people want to contribute
- **High smartphone penetration:** UAE 99%, KSA 97%
- **High disposable income:** GCC users have 6–8× the purchasing power of equivalent Indian users
- **Low incumbent presence:** Splitwise has negligible Arabic UX; Tricount has no Arabic; no local competitor with per-item OCR
- **Expat-heavy population:** Dubai is 90% expat — these users are used to cross-cultural group splitting and are digitally fluent

**Recommended beachhead:** Launch khlaas first in UAE/Dubai market. The brand resonates linguistically, the TAM is high-value, and the competitive gap is widest.

## Market Timing

Three macro forces make 2026–2028 the window:

1. **OCR maturity:** Multimodal LLMs (Gemma 4, Claude Haiku) have crossed the accuracy threshold where receipt scanning actually works reliably on restaurant receipts — this wasn't true at scale before 2024.

2. **Cashless payment normalization:** UPI in India, Tap-to-Pay in GCC, PayNow in Singapore mean users are already comfortable with mobile money; the psychological barrier to settling digitally is gone.

3. **Splitwise stagnation:** Splitwise's last major product innovation was in 2019. Their paid tier ($3.99/month) offers features (receipt scanning with total-only, not per-item) that khlaas ships for free. The window before they copy per-item OCR is 12–18 months.
