## TL;DR for Founders

1. **The Arabic name is your biggest untapped asset.** "Khlaas" (خلاص — "it's done / finished") maps perfectly to the Gulf Arab dining culture where group meals are the social default and bill-splitting is endemic. Start there.

2. **The OCR per-item split is a 10× feature, not a 2× feature.** No competitor does it well on web. This is your moat window — 12–18 months before someone copies it.

3. **Don't charge for the split. Charge for the relationship.** V1 (ephemeral) is your top-of-funnel. V2/V3 (persistent ledger, groups) is where money lives. Design every V1 interaction to pull users toward V2.

4. **Network effects are the endgame.** Splitwise is not a product — it's a debt ledger that holds people hostage. Build the same gravity with groups, running balances, and mutual accountability.

5. **Your three approaches (freemium, per-scan, B2B) are not mutually exclusive — sequence them.** Freemium first (months 1–12), per-scan upsell (months 6–18), B2B as a V3 revenue multiplier (months 18+).
